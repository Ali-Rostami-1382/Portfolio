01 / A LITTLE INTRO

Good work starts
with curiosity.

I like making websites feel simple to use, even when the work behind them isn't simple.

My focus is frontend development: building interfaces that look considered, work across screen sizes, and make sense to the people using them. I'm always experimenting, refining what I know, and finding the next thing worth learning.

Plot twist: I'm also studying Russian language and literature at the University of Tehran. Yes, I know — not exactly the obvious side quest for a frontend developer. But apparently, between debugging JavaScript and decoding Russian literature, I like keeping my brain dramatically overbooked. The bonus? I can watch coding tutorials in Russian and call it both language practice and professional development. You can't exactly pull off that two-for-one deal, can you? 😏
